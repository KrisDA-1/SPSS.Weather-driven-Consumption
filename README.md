## 🧩 Project Hypothesis
Test of Normality
Ho= Energy delta (Wh), Temperature, Pressure, Sunlight Time/Daylength are normally distributed.
H1= Energy delta (Wh), Temperature, Pressure, Sunlight Time/Daylength are not normally distributed.


b.	Kruskal-Wallis (Energy delta vs Weather type)
Ho: The median variation in energy consumption/production is the same across all weather pattern groups.
H1: At least one weather pattern group has a different median variation in energy consumption/production.

c.	Kruskal-Wallis (Energy delta vs temperature)
Ho: The median Energy Delta is the same for all temperature groups.
H1: At least one temperature group has a different median energy delta. 

d.	Kruskal-Wallis (Energy delta vs pressure)
Ho: The median Energy Delta is the same for all pressure groups.
H1: At least one pressure group has a different median energy delta. 

e.	Kruskal-Wallis (Energy delta vs Proportion of sunlight received)
Ho: The median Energy Delta is the same for all Proportion of sunlight groups.
H1: At least one Proportion of sunlight group has a different median energy delta.

f.	GLM (Multiple interactions between variables)
Ho: The is no statistically significant effect of Temperature, pressure and proportion of sun received on Energy Consumption across seasons.
H1: At least one of weather pattern (temperature, pressure and proportion of sun) has a statistically significant effect on energy consumption across seasons.
